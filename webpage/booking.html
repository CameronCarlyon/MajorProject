Cabin Amenities

Explore Emirates' onboard comforts, from in-flight entertainment to multicourse dining.

  • tv_gen

    In-Flight Entertainment

    Emirates' award-winning ICE brings up to 6,500 channels of films, TV, music and games together with moving maps, external cameras and flight updates.

  • restaurant

    Catering Services

    Emirates in-flight meals are built around regionally inspired, multicourse service, with your choice of main, accompaniments, dessert touches and complimentary drinks served throughout the journey.

  • redeem

    Amenity Kit

    Emirates amenity kits include practical travel essentials to help you arrive refreshed, with collectible designs and cabin-specific comfort extras on selected routes.

    Lie-Flat Bed

    Emirates First Class and Business Class seats convert into a fully flat bed across the entire fleet, with turndown touches that make overnight travel noticeably more restful.

    The Emirates A380's onboard Shower Spa lets you arrive refreshed with dedicated VOYA spa products.
